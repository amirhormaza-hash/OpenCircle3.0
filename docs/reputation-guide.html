Your Reputation on OpenCircle

How ratings, badges, and trust levels work — for everyone
Three simple things shape your reputation:
Your Trust Level shows how reliable you are as a member. Your Star Rating shows what other people think of you after sharing an event. Your Badges celebrate the milestones you hit along the way. The better you show up, the more all three grow.
🏅

Your Trust Level

Every member has a Trust Level — it's a running score that goes up when you do good things and down when you don't show up or break the rules. You start at 1,000 points as a brand-new member, which puts you in the Reliable tier right away.

New member tip: You get a 30-day grace period when you first join. If you miss an event or cancel late for the first time, you'll just get a friendly warning instead of losing points — so don't be afraid to dive in.

Your Personal Star Rating

After you attend an event together, other members can give you a 1–5 star rating. This builds your personal reputation score — completely separate from your trust level points. Here's how the labels work once you've collected enough ratings:

🆕 Fewer than 3 ratings New
⭐⭐⭐ 3.0 – 3.9 stars Good
⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.0 – 4.4 stars Great
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.5 – 5.0 stars Excellent
💡
How can you rate someone? Visit their profile after you've both attended the same event. You'll see a rating option — just pick 1 to 5 stars and add some tags. You can rate the same person again if you attend another event together later.
📈
Your recent ratings matter most. The app gives more weight to your most recent 10 ratings. So even if you had a rough start, a run of great events can bring your score back up.
🏷️

Reputation Tags

When someone rates you, they can also choose descriptive tags that describe what it was like spending time with you. These appear as little counters on your profile — a quick snapshot of the kind of person you are at events.

😊 Friendly ✅ Reliable 🎉 Fun 👑 Great Host 📋 Organized 🤝 Welcoming ⚡ Active ✨ Good Vibes 🙏 Respectful ⏰ Punctual

Tags don't change your point score — they're purely a way for the community to vouch for you in their own words. The more events you attend, the more tags you collect.

How to Build a Great Reputation — Fast

Starting from your first day, here's the simplest path to becoming a Trusted member:

Tip 1
Finish your profile. Add a photo and write a short bio. That's +20 points in 2 minutes.
Tip 2
Join events early. Sign up the day before — you get a bonus just for planning ahead.
Tip 3
Always show up. A no-show hits your score hard. If you can't make it, cancel early.
Tip 4
Say hi in the group chat. A quick message earns points and makes you more welcoming.
Tip 5
Rate people after events. You get +5 just for leaving an honest review. Do it every time.
Tip 6
Try hosting once. A well-run event earns you big points and the Host badge.
✅
An active, friendly member can realistically reach Trusted status within 2 to 4 weeks of regular use. You don't need to do anything special — just show up and be a good person.
