Frequently asked

How do I create an event?
Tap the Create tab, add photos, a title, description, category, date, and location, then publish. Your event appears in the feed for others to join.

Why did an event disappear?
Events are meant to be timely. Each one stays visible until 24 hours after its start time, then it's removed from the feed and your list.

How do I rate an event?
If you joined an event, a star rating appears on its card in My List during the 24 hours after it starts.

How do I message someone?
Every event you join has a group chat with the other attendees. You can also open someone's profile and send a direct message.

How do I report a problem or another user?
Long-press any message, or open a user's profile, to report it. Event hosts can also remove attendees from their events.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and associated data.
