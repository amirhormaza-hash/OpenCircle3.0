Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

OpenCircle ("the app," "we," "us") is a mobile application for discovering, joining, and hosting local events. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using OpenCircle you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and the profile details you choose to add: name, username, profile photo, bio, and a general location you type in.

Content you create. Events you host (title, description, photos, address, date, category), messages you send in event chats and direct messages, ratings and reputation tags you give and receive, and reports you submit.

Location. With your permission, we use your device's location to show how far away events are and to sort events by distance. Your device location is used in the app and is not stored on our servers. Event locations are addresses provided by event hosts.

Technical data. Basic information needed to run the service, such as authentication tokens and timestamps of your activity (e.g. when a message was sent).

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: show events near you, let you join and host, chat with attendees, and build your public reputation.
  • To keep the community safe: process reports, enforce our rules, and prevent abuse (including a behavior score used for moderation).
  • To maintain and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

What other users can see

OpenCircle is a social app. Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, badges, streaks, ratings, reputation tags, and event history), the events you host, your attendance in events, and messages you send in event chats are visible to other signed-in users as part of normal app functionality. Direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient.

Service providers

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places API) — when you type an address while creating an event, the text you type is sent to Google to suggest addresses.
  • Expo / Google Play — app distribution and updates.

These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your account and associated data (profile, events, messages, ratings, and badges). You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is protected by row-level security rules so users can only access data the app intends them to see.

Age requirement

OpenCircle is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor is using the app, contact us and we will remove the account.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile information in the app at any time, and delete your account as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access or portability requests) — contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
