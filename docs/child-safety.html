Child Safety Standards

Effective date: July 25, 2026

OpenCircle has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards describe how we prevent, detect, and respond to such content and conduct, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable law.

Our commitment

The safety of minors is a top priority. Any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or abuses a child is strictly prohibited on OpenCircle and will result in immediate removal, a permanent ban, and, where required, reporting to the appropriate authorities.

Age requirement

OpenCircle is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or use the service. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will remove the account.

Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly forbidden anywhere on OpenCircle, including profiles, event listings, photos, group chats, and direct messages:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind;
  • Sexualization of minors, including in text, images, or events;
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to contact or lure a minor for sexual purposes;
  • Trafficking, endangerment, or any exploitation of a child;
  • Sharing or requesting sexual content involving anyone who is or appears to be a minor.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • The app is restricted to adults (18+), stated in our Terms of Service and store listing.
  • Users can report any message, user, event, or piece of content directly in the app.
  • Event hosts can remove attendees, and we can restrict features, suspend, or permanently ban accounts that violate these standards.
  • Reports are reviewed and actioned promptly; violating content is removed and offending accounts are banned.

How to report

Anyone can report suspected child safety violations:

  • In the app — use the report option on any message, user, or event.
  • By email — contact us at amirhormaza@gmail.com with the details.

We act on reports of CSAE as a matter of urgency.

Reporting to authorities

When we become aware of apparent CSAM, we remove it, preserve relevant information as permitted by law, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant authorities in the applicable jurisdiction. We cooperate with law enforcement investigations.

Compliance

These standards align with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable child-protection laws. We review and update them as needed.
